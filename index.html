About

Logistics That Keep Business Moving.

Gavic Express Limited provides courier and logistics solutions designed around the dependable movement of goods and customer service that is easy to reach.

Keeping Your Business Moving.

We are a Zambian courier and logistics company based in Lusaka. The work covers parcels, mail and business documents, door-to-door delivery, and the transportation of goods — including items that need more careful handling. Movements are taken within Lusaka and across Zambia.

Tell us what needs to move, where it is going, and when you would like it collected. We confirm what is possible before anything is booked. Availability, including faster options, is always subject to confirmation.
